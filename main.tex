\begin{abstract}
Deploying white matter hyperintensity (WMH) segmentation on machines
without GPUs motivates model compression. When a 1.98\,M-parameter U-Net
is reduced to 0.12\,M parameters, DSC falls by only 0.010, but
small-lesion recall falls by 0.201, showing that DSC hides a much larger
loss. We compare four compression families and an attention variant at a
matched parameter budget using lesion-size-stratified recall. Removing
the deepest U-Net stage gives the highest small-lesion recall (0.479),
equal within seed variation to a four-times-larger model, while keeping
DSC at the baseline level. Compression studies for WMH segmentation
should therefore report lesion-size-stratified recall alongside DSC.
% 아래 다섯 문장을 영어로 풀면 됩니다.
%  (1) WMH 분할 모델을 GPU 없는 환경에 배포하려면 압축이 필요하다.
%  (2) 그런데 1/16로 줄이면 DSC는 0.010만 떨어지는 반면
%      작은 병변 재현율은 0.201 떨어진다. DSC가 이 손실을 숨긴다.
%  (3) 네 압축 계열과 어텐션을 같은 0.12M 파라미터에서
%      병변 크기별 재현율로 비교했다.
%  (4) 가장 깊은 단계를 제거한 구조가 0.479로 가장 좋았고,
%      4배 큰 모델과 같은 수준이며 DSC는 원본 수준을 유지했다.
%  (5) 경량화 연구는 DSC만 보고해서는 안 된다.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

% 1문단: WMH가 무엇이고 왜 자동 분할이 필요한가.
%        치매·뇌졸중 위험 평가에 쓰이는 지표다.
White matter hyperintensities (WMH) are visible lesions on brain MRI and
are used as imaging markers in dementia and stroke risk assessment.
Manual delineation is time-consuming and variable, so automatic
segmentation is needed for consistent quantification.

% 2문단: 배포 제약. 상위권 방법은 GPU 서버의 앙상블이다.
%        병원 판독실 PC에는 GPU가 없고 영상을 외부로 보내기
%        어려운 경우가 있어 압축 연구가 늘고 있다.
High-performing segmentation systems often rely on ensembles or large
models that assume GPU-side inference. In routine reading rooms, however,
GPU hardware may be unavailable, and sending scans outside the hospital
can be difficult; this motivates compact models for local deployment
\cite{zhu2022vbnet,mahe2026strokeseg,lan2026kd}.

% 3문단 — 문제 제기. 여기가 논문의 핵심입니다.
%        기존 압축 연구는 결과를 DSC 하나로 보고한다.
%        그러나 DSC는 겹친 픽셀 수로 점수를 매기므로 큰 병변이 지배한다.
%        이 데이터에서 작은 병변은 개수로 전체의 37%지만 부피로는 2% 미만이다.
%        실제로 파라미터를 1/16로 줄였을 때 DSC는 0.010만 떨어졌지만
%        작은 병변 재현율은 0.201 떨어졌다. 스무 배 차이다.
The usual summary metric for compression is DSC, but DSC is dominated by
overlapping pixels and therefore by large lesions. In our test set, small
lesions are 37\% of lesions by count but under 2\% by volume. When the
baseline is reduced from 1.98\,M to 0.12\,M parameters, DSC drops by only
0.010, while small-lesion recall drops by 0.201, a 20-fold larger change.

% 4문단 — 연구 질문.
%        그렇다면 같은 파라미터 수로 줄일 때
%        어떤 압축 방식이 작은 병변을 가장 덜 잃는가?
This raises the question studied here: at a matched parameter budget,
which compression strategy loses the fewest small lesions?

The contributions of this paper are threefold:
\begin{itemize}
  \item We show that compression removes small lesions in a way DSC does
        not reveal, by measuring recall stratified by lesion volume.
  \item We compare four compression families and an attention variant
        under a \emph{matched parameter budget} on the same model and data.
  \item We give a practical guideline---remove the deepest stage, keep
        channels at high resolution---together with its compute cost,
        which we report rather than hide.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work}

Compact WMH segmentation has been studied before; for example, VB-Net
reduces the model to 1.08\,M parameters, but does not analyze compression
against an uncompressed counterpart or stratify performance by lesion
size \cite{zhu2022vbnet}. Compression has also been studied for related
segmentation tasks: StrokeSeg2 uses distillation to preserve lesion F1,
and Lan et al. combine $1/4$ channel scaling with output distillation
\cite{mahe2026strokeseg,lan2026kd}. These settings differ from small WMH
segmentation because larger stroke lesions or anatomical structures are
less likely to vanish at coarse resolution. Depth reduction and shallow
U-Net variants are also known \cite{zhou2020unetpp}. Missed
small deep WMH have further been addressed with post-hoc uncertainty
estimation \cite{philps2025}. Our contribution is not to claim these
ideas as new, but to quantify, at a matched parameter budget, which
compression family preserves small lesions best.

\section{Method}

\subsection{Data and Lesion Definition}

We use the MICCAI WMH Segmentation Challenge dataset \cite{kuijf2019wmh}:
170 subjects from three institutions and five scanners, with the
organizers' split of 60 training and 110 test subjects. Two scanners
appear only in the test set. We use FLAIR alone as input.

A lesion is one 26-connected component of the ground-truth mask after
removing label 2 (other pathology). The test set contains 6{,}939
lesions. Volume thresholds were fixed \emph{before} any test result was
examined, by splitting the 3{,}680 training lesions into volume tertiles:
small $<11.0\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, medium $11.0$--$28.1\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, large
$\geq 28.1\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. Small lesions make up 2{,}577 of the test
lesions (37\%). The threshold is defined by volume rather than voxel
count because voxel size varies across scanners: the same
$11\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ spans 2.3 voxels on one scanner and 6.3 on another.

\subsection{Baseline and Matched Parameter Budget}

The baseline is a 2.5D U-Net \cite{ronneberger2015unet} (MONAI \texttt{BasicUNet}) with five stages
and channels 32--32--64--128--256, giving 1.98\,M parameters and 4.98\,G
MACs per $256\!\times\!256$ slice. The input stacks the slice above, the
current slice and the slice below as three channels.
% 2.5D를 쓴 이유: 슬라이스가 3mm로 두꺼워 3D 합성곱의 이득이 작고,
% 파라미터·메모리 증가가 경량화 목적과 맞지 않는다.
We use 2.5D rather than 3D because slices are 3\,mm thick, limiting the
benefit of through-plane convolution, while 3D models increase parameters
and memory in conflict with the compression goal.

% 학습 설정: 60 epoch, AdamW (lr 1e-3, wd 1e-4), cosine, BCE+Dice,
% 라벨 2 제외, 병변 슬라이스 3배 샘플링, 검증 9명 DSC 최대 epoch 선택.
Models are trained for 60 epochs with AdamW (learning rate $10^{-3}$,
weight decay $10^{-4}$), a cosine schedule, and BCE+Dice loss after
excluding label 2. Lesion-containing slices are sampled three times more
often, and the checkpoint is selected by the best DSC on the nine-subject
validation split.

Every compressed model is held to the same parameter count, about
0.12\,M, one sixteenth of the baseline. If models differ in size the
larger one wins by default and the question ``which method loses less''
cannot be asked. At a fixed budget, any difference reflects \emph{where}
the parameters were spent. Parameter count alone is not a fair cost
measure: convolution parameters scale with the square of the channel
count and are independent of spatial size, while MACs scale with channels
squared \emph{times} spatial size. We therefore report MACs and measured
CPU time alongside.

\subsection{Compression Families}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Structural, width.} All channels scaled to $1/4$ (W0.25;
        8 channels at the first stage).
  \item \textbf{Structural, depth.} The deepest stage is removed and the
        remaining stages widened to 16 channels at the first stage.
  \item \textbf{Pruning.} L1-norm filter pruning \cite{li2017pruning},
        staged $32\!\to\!24\!\to\!16\!\to\!8$ with retraining at each step.
  \item \textbf{Quantization.} Post-training INT8 (ONNX QDQ), 256
        calibration slices, per-channel MinMax, no retraining.
  \item \textbf{Distillation.} Teacher W1.0 to student, logit
        distillation at $\tau=2$ with a per-lesion weight
        $\sqrt{V_{\mathrm{med}}/V}$ clipped to $[1,4]$ \cite{hinton2015distilling}.
  \item \textbf{Attention gates.} Attention U-Net gates
        \cite{oktay2018attention} on every skip connection
        ($+3.5\%$ parameters).
\end{itemize}

Pruning, quantization and distillation operate on an existing model,
whereas structural changes and attention alter the design itself. We do
not combine two changes at once, since the contribution of each could not
then be separated.

\subsection{Evaluation}

Predictions are mapped back to the original resolution and scored with
the official challenge definitions. The operating threshold is fixed at
0.5 for every model. We report DSC, absolute volume difference (AVD),
lesion F1, and recall stratified by lesion size. Recall cannot be read
alone---a model that paints the entire image attains recall 1.0---so
every claimed recall gain is checked against AVD and lesion precision.
Core models use three seeds; models marked $\dagger$ use one and are
excluded from every conclusion.

Table~\ref{tab:main} ranks the matched-budget models by small-lesion

distillation and the model produced by pruning are both, in effect,
U-Nets with reduced channel capacity.

\subsection{Why the Deepest Stage Can Go}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{fig1_architecture_bc.pdf}
\caption{Two networks at the same parameter count (0.12\,M). Left:
uniform width scaling (W0.25). Right: the deepest stage removed and the
remaining stages widened. The first stage, where small lesions are still
visible, carries 8 versus 16 channels.}
\label{fig:arch}
\end{figure}

% 기전 3문장: U-Net은 단계마다 영상을 절반으로 줄인다
% (256 -> 128 -> 64 -> 32 -> 16). 3복셀 병변은 16x16에서 0.2복셀이 되어
% 이미 없다. 즉 가장 깊은 단계는 작은 병변에 대한 정보를 갖고 있지 않은데,
% 파라미터는 그곳이 전체의 약 1/3이다.
Fig.~\ref{fig:arch} shows how the two matched-budget structures differ.
The mechanism is consistent with the U-Net resolution schedule. Each
downsampling stage halves the image size (256, 128, 64, 32, and
16 pixels), so a 3-voxel lesion occupies only about 0.2 voxel at the
$16\!\times\!16$ stage. The deepest stage therefore has little spatial
evidence for small lesions, yet it contains about one third of the
parameters.

% 블록 민감도 1문장: 원본에서 블록 하나씩 필터 절반을 잘라 DSC 변화를
% 재면, 가장 깊은 층은 656k를 잘라도 -0.002인 반면
% 가장 얕은 층은 20k만 잘라도 -0.11이다.
A block-sensitivity check on the baseline supports the same interpretation:
halving filters in the deepest block removes 656k parameters but changes
DSC by only 0.002, whereas halving filters in the shallowest block removes
about 20k parameters and changes DSC by 0.11.

Compared against a model four times larger, depth reduction (0.13\,M,
1.08\,G) reaches $0.479 \pm 0.009$ while W0.5 (0.49\,M, 1.26\,G) reaches
$0.470 \pm 0.003$---equal within seed variation, at one quarter the size
and lower compute.
% 1문장: 깊이 축소 모델은 W0.5에서 가장 깊은 단계만 떼어낸 것이므로,
% 이는 그 단계가 파라미터만 차지하고 일을 하지 않았다는 직접 증거다.
Because this model is obtained from W0.5 by removing only the deepest
stage, this comparison is direct evidence that the removed stage was
mostly occupying parameters rather than contributing useful small-lesion
evidence.

\subsection{Training Tricks Do Not Substitute for Structure}

% 증류 2문장: 폭 축소 위에서는 0.366 -> 0.420으로 회복하지만
% AVD가 25.2%에서 33.3%로 커진다. 깊이 축소 위에 얹으면
% 0.479 -> 0.466으로 늘지 않고 AVD(27.6%)와 seed 편차(0.040)만 커진다.
% 증류는 구조적 결함을 메우는 기법이므로 구조를 고치면 메울 것이 남지 않는다.
Distillation helps the poorly allocated W0.25 student, increasing
small-lesion recall from 0.366 to 0.420, but at the cost of volume
accuracy: AVD rises from 25.2\% to 33.3\%. When added to the
depth-reduced structure, distillation gives no recall gain (0.479 to
0.466) and increases both AVD (27.6\%) and seed variation (0.040). Thus,
distillation partly compensates for a structural deficit, but it does not
substitute for allocating parameters to the stages where small lesions
remain visible.

% 어텐션 2문장: 사전에 "차이 0.02 미만이면 무승부"로 정해 두었고
% 실제 차이는 0.014였다. 세 seed 모두 재현율이 올랐으나 정밀도가
% 0.642에서 0.631로 떨어져 F1은 그대로다. 파라미터를 3.5% 더 쓰고
% 얻은 것이 없다.
For attention gates, we used a pre-registered criterion: a difference
below 0.02 was treated as a tie, whereas a difference of at least 0.03
was treated as meaningful. The observed recall gap was 0.014, so attention
provides no significant gain by this criterion. Although recall increased
for all three seeds, precision fell from 0.642 to 0.631 and lesion F1
remained essentially unchanged, despite using 3.5\% more parameters.

\subsection{Compute Cost}

% 3문장: 깊이 축소는 파라미터는 같지만 MACs가 폭 축소의 3.4배이고,
% 실측 CPU 시간은 환자당 1.4초에서 4초로 2.8배다. 다만
% (a) 같은 성능의 W0.5보다는 빠르고,
% (b) 계산량까지 맞춘 모델(0.03M, 0.28G)도 0.418로 폭 축소(0.366)를 이긴다.
% 즉 가장 깊은 단계를 빼는 것 자체는 계산량 대가 없이 이득이고,
% 0.418에서 0.479로 더 올리는 부분이 계산량을 지불하고 사는 것이다.
Depth reduction has the same parameter budget as W0.25 but uses 3.4 times
more MACs (1.08\,G vs. 0.32\,G), and measured CPU time increases from
about 1.4 to 4 seconds per subject. This cost should not be hidden, but
two checks qualify it: the model remains lower-compute than the
performance-matched W0.5 model (1.08\,G vs. 1.26\,G), and a
compute-matched depth-reduced variant (0.03\,M, 0.28\,G; single seed,
reference only) still reaches 0.418 small-lesion recall versus 0.366 for
W0.25. Thus, removing the deepest stage itself gives a gain without a
compute penalty; the additional increase from 0.418 to 0.479 is the part
bought with extra computation.

\section{Discussion}

% 1문단 — 함의. 압축할 때 작은 병변을 지키려면 파라미터를 어디에 써야
% 하는가에 대한 답은 "작은 병변이 아직 보이는 고해상도 단계의 채널에
% 써라"이다. 학습 기법이나 추가 기구는 그다음이다.
The practical implication is that, under a tight parameter budget,
compression should spend capacity where small lesions are still spatially
visible. In this U-Net family, that means keeping channels in the
high-resolution stages and removing the deepest stage, rather than first
shrinking every stage uniformly and relying on training tricks or added
modules to recover the loss.

% 2문단 — 보고 관행. 경량화 연구가 DSC만 보고하면 이 손실이 드러나지
% 않는다. 병변 크기별 재현율을 함께 보고할 것을 제안한다.
The reporting implication is equally important. If lightweight WMH
models are evaluated only by DSC, a model can appear nearly unchanged
while losing many small lesions. We therefore recommend reporting
lesion-size-stratified recall together with DSC, with AVD or lesion
precision used to check that higher recall is not obtained by simply
painting more voxels.

% 3문단 — 한계. 데이터셋 1개, U-Net 계열 1개, FLAIR 단일 입력.
% 임상 결과와의 연관성은 검증하지 않았다. 계열당 대표 기법 하나씩만
% 다루었으며 QAT와 특징 증류는 향후 과제다. 실기기 속도는 CPU 한
% 기종에서만 측정했다.
This study has several limitations. It uses one dataset, one U-Net family,
and FLAIR as the only input sequence, and it does not test whether the
additional small lesions change clinical decisions. We also evaluate one
representative method per compression family; quantization-aware training
and feature distillation remain future work. Measured runtime is reported
on one CPU platform, so deployment speed should be remeasured for other
hardware.

\section{Conclusion}

% 4~5문장: 무엇을 줄이느냐가 결정적이다. 같은 0.12M에서 깊은 단계를 빼고
% 얕은 층을 지킨 구조가 0.479로 폭 축소(0.366), 프루닝(0.397),
% 증류(0.420)를 모두 앞섰고 4배 큰 모델과 동급이며 DSC는 원본 수준을
% 유지했다. 대가는 계산량이며 환자당 약 4초다.
% 경량화 연구는 DSC만 보고해서는 안 된다.
What is removed matters as much as how much is removed. At the same
0.12\,M-parameter budget, removing the deepest stage and preserving
high-resolution channels reaches 0.479 small-lesion recall, above width
scaling (0.366), pruning (0.397), and distillation (0.420). It is equal
within seed variation to a four-times-larger W0.5 model and keeps DSC at
the baseline level, but the price is higher computation: about 4 seconds
per subject on CPU. Lightweight WMH segmentation should not be judged by
DSC alone.
